Ben's TV Show Reviews

Hi, my name is Ben and I enjoy watching drama and thriller television series. I am particularly drawn to TV series that have complex plots that are able to producing endless episodes.On this page, I share some of my favorite TV shows and my thoughts about each one.

Succession

Platform: HBO

Rating: 5/5

Succession is one of my favorite TV shows because of the intense family dynamics and amazing acting performances. The show follows the Roy family as they compete for control of a powerful media company. The series explores dark family secrets and the toxic competition created by a narcissistic father. The show is full of twists and turns and is the perfect mix of drama, comedy, and suspense. It is a must-watch for anyone who enjoys family dramas.

The Handmaid's Tale

Platform: Hulu

Rating: 4/5

The Handmaid's Tale is a dystopian drama based on a fanatic interpretation of the Bible where women are marginalized and oppressed. The show is a surreal depiction of a totalitarian society that is out of control. Women are treated as property and serve in a reproductive capacity to the upper class of this society. The show is riveting with awesome acting and lots of twists and turns in the plot! You have to watch it!

Homeland

Platform: Showtime

Rating: 4/5

Homeland is a political and psychological thriller that follows the interesting story of a schizophrenic CIA operative who is deeply entanlged in the dangerous world of espionage. The show is known for its intense scenes and amazing actors. Each episde could be a surprise and potentially represent its own independent movie.
